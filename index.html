How to Protect Yourself

Simple habits that can reduce your risk of online scams.

01
🔗

Check Links

Carefully check the domain before opening an unfamiliar link.

02
🔐

Never Share OTP

Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or verification codes with unexpected callers or messages.

03
📧

Verify the Sender

Check the actual email address, not just the displayed name.

04
📱

Use Trusted Apps

Prefer official app stores and verify the developer before installing.

05
⚠️

Don't Panic

Scammers often create urgency. Stop and independently verify.

06
💳

Verify Payments

Don't transfer money because of an unexpected message or call.
